# How I like my cuts

Read this before planning any cut. It wins over the defaults in SKILL.md. Step 7 of SKILL.md ("Teach my /cut") rewrites it
from my review comments, so it grows with every video: keep it to principles with their reason, short enough to read in a
minute, and fold a new lesson into the line it changes instead of adding a dated entry.

## Starting defaults

- Keep the last clean take of every line, played whole.
- Cut false starts, retake markers and long pauses between lines (over about 0.6 s).
- Leave about 0.1 s of air before the first word and after the last word of every clip.
- Keep everything else I say, including asides and signposts.
